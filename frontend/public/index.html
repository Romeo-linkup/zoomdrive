Install the apps

ZoomDrive is for drivers. ZoomDrive Partner is for service providers. Each installs separately and shows as its own icon.
Android (Chrome) Open the app, then tap the menu and choose Install app.
iPhone (Safari) Open the app, tap the Share button, then choose Add to Home Screen.
